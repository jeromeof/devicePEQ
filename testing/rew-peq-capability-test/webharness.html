Automated capability test

For each supported filter type (PK always; LSQ/HSQ if the device's peqConstraints profile flags them), pushes maxGain/minGain/maxQ test cases, brackets each with a fresh flat baseline immediately before and after (to surface measurement drift rather than let it contaminate results), and statistically compares the measured response against the theoretical filter shape.

CaseReadbackPregainShape RMSEMax ErrCorrDriftVerdict

Pregain is the median broadband offset between the measured and theoretical delta curves — most devices back off their overall gain automatically when a boost filter is applied (headroom protection), which shows up as a constant negative offset across the whole band, not just at the filter's own frequency. It's subtracted out before Shape RMSE / Max Err / Corr are computed, so those three describe how well the filter's actual *shape* matches the theoretical biquad once the pregain is accounted for — the real acoustic pass/fail criterion.
